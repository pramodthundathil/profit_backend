Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how PRO-FIT Gym ("we", "us", or "our"), developed and maintained by ByteBoot Techno Solutions Pvt Ltd, collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information when you use our mobile application and web portal.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to our gym members, trainers, and administrators:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, mobile phone number, profile photo, gender, date of birth, and emergency contact details.
  • Gym & Membership Data: Assigned gym branch, subscription plan, start/expiry dates, attendance logs, and payment receipts.
  • Health & Activity Data: Food logs, calorie intake, workout records, and physical measurements (height, weight) voluntarily entered by you or your trainer.
  • Device & Usage Data: Device model, operating system version, push notification tokens, and application interaction logs.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To manage gym memberships, attendance, and branch access control.
  • To authenticate users via OTP (SMS/Email) and secure JWT authentication.
  • To track dietary logs, trainer assignments, and workout progress.
  • To send important push notifications, payment reminders, and gym announcements.
  • To provide customer support and troubleshoot technical issues.

3. Data Sharing & Security

We take the security of your personal data seriously:

  • No Sale of Data: We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties or advertisers.
  • Gym Administration Access: Your personal profile and membership details are accessible only to the authorized staff and trainers of your subscribed gym office.
  • Data Encryption: All sensitive communication between the mobile app and server is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS/SSL protocols.

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your personal data for as long as your gym membership remains active or as needed to comply with legal obligations. You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting your gym administration or emailing support@byteboot.in.

5. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for individuals who meet the minimum age requirements set by their respective gym facilities. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date above.
